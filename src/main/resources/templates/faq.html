Frequently Asked Questions

💰 What is a crypto faucet?

A crypto faucet is a website that gives out small amounts of cryptocurrency for free, typically in exchange for completing simple tasks or captcha verifications.

🔒 Is Spiriponzi safe?

Yes, Spiriponzi uses secure protocols and does not require any sensitive information beyond your email address for registration.

🕓 How often can I claim points?

You can claim points once every 60 minutes. Check back regularly to maximize your earnings!

💸 How do I withdraw crypto?

Once you accumulate 3,000 points, you can request a withdrawal. We currently support Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH).

🔁 Can I refer friends?

Yes! Share your referral link to earn bonus points every time someone signs up through it and starts claiming.

📬 I didn’t receive my withdrawal, what should I do?

Withdrawals are processed within 48 hours. If it's been longer, please contact our support team via email or Telegram.
